# Exterior Porch Painting Estimate

**Project:** Back Porch Painting + Front Porch Floor
**Property:** 5933 NE 31st Ave, Portland, OR  
**Customer:** Camille Omey

**Prepared:** 2026-08-11

## Project Summary

The main project is preparation and painting of the newly constructed back porch and lower enclosure at 5933 NE 31st Ave. The back porch red floor scope is approximately **15 ft x 6 ft**, or **90 sq ft total**, including the stairs and landing. The back porch ceiling/roof underside is approximately **15 ft x 8 ft**, or **120 sq ft**. The new lower enclosure will be green; the back porch floor will be red; and the ceiling, posts, beams, braces, fascia, and trim will be cream.

This estimate also includes preparation and repainting of the existing **front porch floor**. The visible coating is peeling and areas of wood are exposed, so preparation beyond a simple recoat is included.

Final colors will be confirmed by the customer before materials are purchased.

## Scope of Work

### Protection and Preparation

- Protect the house, landscaping, door, window, and adjacent surfaces.
- Clean and prepare all included surfaces for coating.
- Fill exposed fastener holes and caulk appropriate joints in the new plywood enclosure.
- Prime bare plywood, new lumber, and repaired areas with compatible exterior primer.
- Mask and maintain clean transitions where the new construction meets the existing house.

### Painting

- Apply two finish coats of green exterior paint to the visible exterior faces of the new porch base/enclosure.
- Apply the appropriate red porch-and-floor coating to the approximately 90 sq ft total floor scope, including the stairs and landing without adding their area a second time.
- Clean the front porch floor.
- Scrape loose and flaking paint from the front porch floor.
- Sand and feather coating edges as appropriate, then remove preparation dust.
- Spot-prime exposed bare wood on the front porch floor.
- Apply two finish coats of the approved red porch-and-floor coating to the front porch floor and any floor edge expressly visible and intended for red paint.
- Apply two finish coats of cream exterior paint to the porch ceiling, posts, beams, braces, new fascia, and associated trim.
- Complete normal minor touch-ups at the junction between the new construction and the existing house.
- Complete demasking, cleanup, and a final walkthrough.

Normal labor, paint, primer, sundries, masking, setup, and cleanup are included.

## Assumptions

- The plywood enclosure will remain the finished exterior surface.
- Only visible exterior surfaces are included. The crawlspace and interior enclosure walls are excluded.
- The 90 sq ft red floor measurement applies to the back porch and already includes its visible stairs and landing. The front porch floor is a separate included add-on.
- Front porch work is limited to the porch floor and any floor edge expressly visible and intended for red paint. Front porch railings, columns, siding, and trim are excluded.
- New construction is complete, dry, secure, and ready for normal paint preparation before work begins.
- Final colors and products will be approved before purchase.

## Exclusions

- Carpentry, siding installation, structural repair, or replacement of deteriorated fascia.
- Extensive preparation of the existing peeling fascia. Its visible coating failure requires separate on-site evaluation before any additional work is authorized.
- Repair or replacement of split, loose, rotten, or structurally deteriorated front porch boards.
- Front porch railings, columns, siding, trim, and carpentry.
- Painting crawlspace surfaces, interior enclosure walls, roofing, gutters, glass, screens, door hardware, or landscaping.
- Repair of hidden damage, moisture problems, failed substrates, or construction defects.
- Additional coating caused by customer color changes after material purchase or by conditions outside normal product coverage.

## Schedule

Expected project duration is approximately **2-3 working days** for a two-person crew if the front porch work can be completed concurrently with back porch drying and recoating. Timing remains subject to weather, drying and cure times, final colors/products, and field conditions.

## Total Estimate

- **Back porch painting: $2,100**
- **Front porch floor preparation and painting: $400**
- **Total estimate: $2,500**

This price is based on the supplied photos, approximate dimensions, visible conditions, and the scope above. A written change order is required for work outside this scope.
