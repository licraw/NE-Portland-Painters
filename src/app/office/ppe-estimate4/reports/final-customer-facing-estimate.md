# Exterior Porch Painting Estimate

**Project:** 5933 NE 31st Ave - Covered Porch / Enclosure Painting  
**Property:** 5933 NE 31st Ave, Portland, OR  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-11

## Project Summary

This estimate covers preparation and painting of the newly constructed covered porch and lower enclosure at 5933 NE 31st Ave. The upper deck is approximately **15 ft x 6 ft**, and the covered ceiling surface is estimated at approximately **90-110 sq ft**. The new lower enclosure will be green, the porch floor and visible stairs red, and the ceiling, posts, beams, braces, fascia, and trim cream.

Final colors will be confirmed by the customer before materials are purchased.

## Scope of Work

### Protection and Preparation

- Protect the house, landscaping, door, window, and adjacent surfaces.
- Clean and prepare all included surfaces for coating.
- Fill exposed fastener holes and caulk appropriate joints in the new plywood enclosure.
- Prime bare plywood, new lumber, and repaired areas with compatible exterior primer.
- Mask and maintain clean transitions where the new construction meets the existing house.

### Painting

- Apply two finish coats of green exterior paint to the visible exterior faces of the new porch base/enclosure.
- Apply the appropriate red porch-and-floor coating to the approximately 90 sq ft upper deck, seven visible stair treads, and visible paintable stair risers.
- Apply two finish coats of cream exterior paint to the porch ceiling, posts, beams, braces, new fascia, and associated trim.
- Complete normal minor touch-ups at the junction between the new construction and the existing house.
- Complete demasking, cleanup, and a final walkthrough.

Normal labor, paint, primer, sundries, masking, setup, and cleanup are included.

## Assumptions

- The plywood enclosure will remain the finished exterior surface.
- Only visible exterior surfaces are included. The crawlspace and interior enclosure walls are excluded.
- The red floor scope includes the upper porch deck and the seven clearly visible stairs; no additional landing is included.
- New construction is complete, dry, secure, and ready for normal paint preparation before work begins.
- Final colors and products will be approved before purchase.

## Exclusions

- Carpentry, siding installation, structural repair, or replacement of deteriorated fascia.
- Extensive preparation of the existing peeling fascia. Its visible coating failure requires separate on-site evaluation before any additional work is authorized.
- Scraping, sanding, or disturbance of suspected pre-1978 coatings. No lead-safe remediation or containment allowance is included in this estimate.
- Painting crawlspace surfaces, interior enclosure walls, roofing, gutters, glass, screens, door hardware, or landscaping.
- Repair of hidden damage, moisture problems, failed substrates, or construction defects.
- Additional coating caused by customer color changes after material purchase or by conditions outside normal product coverage.

## Schedule

Expected project duration is approximately **2-3 working days** for a two-person crew, depending on weather, drying and cure times, final colors/products, and field conditions.

## Total Estimate

**Total covered porch painting estimate: $2,100**

This price is based on the supplied photos, approximate dimensions, visible conditions, and the scope above. A written change order is required for work outside this scope.
