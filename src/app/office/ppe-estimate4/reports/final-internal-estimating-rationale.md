**Purpose:** Internal estimating record. Not customer facing.

## Recommended Price

**Recommended customer price: $2,100**

The price supports a two-painter, approximately 2-3 day production model with separate primer, green exterior, cream exterior, and red porch-and-floor coating purchases. It includes ordinary exterior protection, new-construction preparation, detailed framing, overhead ceiling work, visible stairs, setup, cleanup, and the established business markup. The preliminary $2,950 placeholder was rejected after comparison with the completed 7610 SE Harrison shed/studio estimate and the 1707 SE Keanu half-bath estimate.

## Scope Basis

The working takeoff uses an approximately 15 ft x 6 ft upper deck, 90 sq ft deck area, approximately 90-110 sq ft of ceiling (105 sq ft pricing allowance), approximately 150 sq ft of visible enclosure, seven stairs, and approximately 220 sq ft of converted cream framing/trim area. The separately supplied 8 ft ceiling dimension is surface width, not porch height. Measurements are photo-based and rounded for production.

The exterior enclosure is assumed complete and suitable to remain as the finished surface. Only visible exterior faces are included. Carpentry, enclosure interiors, crawlspace surfaces, structural repair, and deteriorated-fascia work are not carried.

## Pricing Math

```text
Direct Labor Cost = 40 hours x $25/hour = $1,000
Material and Sundry Allowance = $600
Direct Project Cost = $1,600

Overhead / Profit / Business Markup = $1,600 x 29.4% = $470.40
Calculated Estimate = $1,600 + $470.40 = $2,070.40
Rounded Final Customer Price = $2,100
```

The rounded price leaves **$500 above estimated direct cost**, or approximately **23.81% of the customer price**. As in the Harrison estimate, this combined contribution covers overhead, contingency, and profit; the reference documents do not reliably split those categories. The extra $29.60 created by rounding above the exact 29.4% formula is a modest contingency for product coverage and outdoor scheduling.

## Production and Coating Notes

1. Confirm dimensions, stair count, and color boundaries before mobilization.
2. Verify construction is complete and moisture content meets product requirements.
3. Protect the house, door, window, landscaping, irrigation, and adjacent ground.
4. Clean included surfaces and remove ordinary construction dust/contamination.
5. Fill exposed fastener holes where appropriate; do not conceal required drainage or movement details.
6. Apply compatible paintable exterior caulk only at appropriate joints.
7. Prime bare plywood, lumber, and repaired areas. Seal exposed plywood edges as the selected system permits.
8. Apply two green exterior finish coats to visible enclosure faces.
9. Apply two red porch-and-floor coats to the upper deck and seven stair treads/visible risers, respecting recoat and foot-traffic cure times.
10. Apply two cream exterior finish coats to ceiling, posts, beams, braces, fascia, rails, and associated trim.
11. Complete minor touch-ups at new-to-existing junctions without expanding into existing-house repainting.
12. Demask, inspect, clean, label retained paint, and document final products/colors.

## Lead and Existing Fascia Constraint

The supplied photos show extensive peeling coating on existing fascia. The property age is unknown, and the Estimate 3 documents do not authorize lead-safe work for this project. The base estimate therefore does **not** promise scraping, sanding, or other disturbance of suspected pre-1978 coatings. Existing fascia must be separately evaluated for substrate condition, replacement needs, property age, and any applicable lead-safe requirements before scope or price is added.

## Risk Notes and Potential Change Orders

- Plywood or lumber that is wet, resinous, contaminated, incomplete, or not coating-ready.
- Additional siding installation, fastener correction, carpentry, structural repair, or access-door work.
- Extensive joint filling, exposed voids, delamination, or substrate repair beyond normal new-construction preparation.
- Existing peeling fascia preparation, deterioration, replacement, testing, containment, or lead-safe work.
- Hidden moisture damage or failed existing coatings discovered during normal preparation.
- Additional stairs, landings, rail components, interior/crawlspace faces, or other surfaces not shown in the supplied photos.
- Color changes after purchase, or extra coats required by a highly translucent selected color outside normal coverage.
- Weather or site-access delays beyond normal scheduling flexibility.
