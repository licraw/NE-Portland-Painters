Scope and Measurement Basis

Condition and Preparation

  • Porch floor: coating is worn and marked but substantially adhered. Wash, spot scrape/sand, minor repair, full tinted primer, and one gray finish coat.
  • Stair tread tops: visibly weathered with broad coating loss. Wash and remove loose material; sand only raised, splintered, failed, or repair areas rather than restoring every tread to uniformly bare wood. Full tinted primer provides the uniform base.
  • White rails: localized failure is concentrated at lower rail ends and isolated defects. Scrape/feather and spot-prime those areas; do not universally degloss sound coating unless the selected product’s adhesion test requires it.
  • Risers: generally sound, with one raw replacement riser requiring primer. Minor joint repair only where paintable and non-structural.
  • Split/soft/rotten wood, replacement of caps/treads/boards, structural work, and concealed deterioration remain excluded.

Field Verification and Scope Boundaries

Before purchase or scheduling, field-measure porch length/depth, stair width/count, rail footage, balusters, and posts. Confirm coating compatibility and moisture requirements. Confirm in writing that stringer/skirt faces and the deck rim band are excluded; add them only by written authorization after color and condition are established. Confirm the customer can provide an alternate entry during coating and cure.
