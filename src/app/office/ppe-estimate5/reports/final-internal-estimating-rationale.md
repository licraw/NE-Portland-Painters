**Prepared:** 2026-08-24  
**Purpose:** Internal estimating record; not customer facing.

## Recommendation

- **Labor:** 24 crew hours
- **Materials and sundries allowance:** $225
- **Internal pricing floor:** **$1,350**
- **Fixed customer price:** **$1,450**
- **Crew plan:** two painters for approximately 1.5 active crew-days, normally scheduled across 2 working days for washing, dry-down, recoat, and walking-surface cure

This is a professional pre-listing maintenance repaint. It is smaller and materially simpler than the 5933 NE 31st front-and-back porch project: one complete finish coat rather than two, two colors rather than three, no ceiling/overhead work, no cedar-shake enclosure, no widespread new-construction priming, and no included rim/stringer scope.

## Production Sequence

1. Confirm colors, included components, existing-coating compatibility, and alternate-entry plan.
2. Protect adjacent siding, door/casing, concrete, lattice, landscaping, and other excluded surfaces.
3. Wash included surfaces and allow adequate dry-down.
4. Scrape, feather-sand, fill, and repair only actual failures and minor paintable defects. Remove dust.
5. Apply one complete tinted primer coat to all gray walking surfaces.
6. Spot-prime the raw/repaired/bare white areas only.
7. Apply one complete white finish coat to included risers, railings, balusters, posts, and caps.
8. Apply one complete gray finish coat to porch floor, tread tops, and nosings.
9. Complete color transitions and normal touch-ups, demask, clean, and walk through.

## Warranty Position

Use a limited **two-year workmanship warranty for application defects on the work performed**, provided the final written warranty expressly excludes failure of existing/underlying coatings, concealed deterioration, moisture intrusion, rot, movement, structural defects, and pre-existing substrate conditions. The warranty must not imply restoration-level substrate correction or guarantee the remaining life of coatings not removed.

## Risks, Exclusions, and Field Checks

- Field-measure porch length/depth, stair width/count, rail footage, balusters, and posts before scheduling.
- Confirm stringer/skirt faces and rim band remain excluded; price by written change order only if requested.
- Confirm adhesion/compatibility of existing coatings and selected primer/finishes.
- Probe visibly damaged rail ends and split caps. Wood replacement, structural/carpentry work, rot repair, and board/tread replacement are excluded.
- Exclude lattice, ceiling/soffit, siding, front door/casing, adjacent trim, concrete steps/pad/walkway, undersides, and hidden faces.
- Schedule remains weather-, dry-down-, recoat-, and cure-dependent. No labor is carried for a lack of alternate entry or extraordinary access restrictions.
