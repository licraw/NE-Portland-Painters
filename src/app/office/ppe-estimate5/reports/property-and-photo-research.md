## Scope Conclusions

| Surface | Current condition | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Porch/deck walking surface | Existing gray coating largely intact; worn and locally marked | Wash; localized scrape/sand/repair; one complete tinted primer; one gray finish coat |

| Stringer/skirt faces | Visible but not clearly included in confirmed white assignment | Excluded unless separately authorized |
| Deck rim/skirt band | Visible but not clearly included in confirmed white assignment | Excluded unless separately authorized |
| Lattice, ceiling/soffit, siding, door/casing/trim, concrete | Outside confirmed scope | Protect only; exclude from coating |

## Estimating Conclusion

The correct intent is a targeted pre-listing maintenance repaint. The rail and baluster detail still controls application time, but the coating condition does not justify a restoration package, universal deglossing, spray-grade masking, or two complete finish coats. The full gray primer supplies the consistent base required on walking surfaces; white primer is limited to bare and repaired areas. A two-painter, approximately two-working-day plan is proportionate to the photographed scope and remains materially below the more difficult prior front-and-back porch project.

## Field Verification

Measure porch length/depth, stair width/count, rail footage, balusters, and posts. Confirm product compatibility and moisture limits. Confirm in writing that stringer/skirt faces and rim bands remain excluded, and verify no carpentry or substrate deterioration must be resolved before coating.
