Exterior Porch and Stairs Maintenance Repaint Estimate

Property: 10375 SW Dunlin Pl, Beaverton, OR 97007

Prepared: 2026-08-24

Project Summary

This estimate provides a targeted maintenance repaint of the front porch and entry stairs to create a clean, consistent, market-ready appearance before the property is listed. The work refreshes the existing coated surfaces, corrects localized coating failures and minor paintable defects, and applies a coordinated white-and-gray coating system.

The photo-based working dimensions are an approximately 18 ft x 5 ft porch and an approximately 3.5 ft-wide stair with nine tread tops and ten risers. These dimensions will be confirmed before scheduling.

Included Work

Protection and localized preparation

  • Protect adjacent house siding, front door and casing, threshold, lattice, concrete, walkway, landscaping, and other surfaces not being painted.
  • Wash the included surfaces and allow suitable dry-down.
  • Scrape and feather-sand loose or failing coating only where needed.
  • Sand rough, raised, splintered, or repaired areas as needed for a sound paintable surface; sound existing coatings are retained.
  • Complete minor paintable filling and repairs where appropriate. Drainage gaps and required movement joints remain open.
  • Remove preparation dust before coating.

Gray surfaces

  • Stair tread tops and nosings.
  • Porch/deck walking surface.
  • Apply one complete tinted primer coat to these included gray walking surfaces.
  • Apply one complete gray porch-and-floor finish coat.

White surfaces

  • Ten stair risers, including the raw replacement riser.
  • Both stair railings.
  • Porch guardrail.
  • Associated railing members, balusters, posts, and caps.
  • Spot-prime bare, scraped, repaired, or raw white areas only.
  • Apply one complete white exterior finish coat to all included white surfaces.

Completion

  • Detail the transitions between white and gray and complete normal touch-ups.
  • Remove protection and masking, clean the work area, and complete a final walkthrough.
  • Label and leave usable project coating for future touch-ups.

Normal labor, coatings, minor paintable repair materials, protection, and cleanup are included.

Exclusions

  • Full stripping, restoration, or universal heavy sanding of sound coatings.
  • Structural work, carpentry, rot repair, and replacement of treads, boards, caps, rails, or other wood components.
  • Stair stringer/skirt faces and deck rim/skirt bands. These are not included unless specifically authorized in writing after the desired color boundary is confirmed.
  • Lattice and all surfaces behind it.
  • Porch ceiling or soffit.
  • House siding, front door, door casing, threshold, and adjacent house trim.
  • Aggregate concrete steps, concrete pad, walkway, and asphalt/concrete surfaces.
  • Stair or porch undersides, concealed faces, and deck framing.
  • Correction of moisture intrusion, concealed deterioration, construction defects, or failure within existing underlying coatings.
  • Landscaping pruning or removal; plants will be protected or tied back as practical.

If inspection reveals deteriorated or structurally unsound wood, that area will be brought to the customer’s attention. No additional work will proceed without written approval.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Remove furniture, the crate, doormat, planters, and personal items from the porch before work begins.
  • Provide access to an exterior water source and electrical outlet.
  • Provide an alternate entry while the front stairs and porch are being coated and curing.
  • Keep people and pets off coated surfaces for the product’s stated cure period.
  • Approve the final white and gray colors before material purchase.

Schedule

Expected production is approximately two working days with a two-person crew. Weather, wash dry-down, product recoat windows, and walking-surface cure requirements may affect the sequence or elapsed schedule. We will coordinate access so disruption is kept as short as the coating system permits.
