Exterior Porch and Stairs Painting Estimate

Property: 10375 SW Dunlin Pl, Beaverton, OR 97007

Prepared: 2026-08-24

Project Summary

This estimate covers preparation and repainting of the front porch and entry stairs. The goal is to give the entry a clean, refreshed appearance before the property is listed.

The porch floor, stair treads, and nosings will be gray. The stair risers, stair railings, porch guardrail, balusters, posts, and caps will be white. Final colors will be confirmed before paint is purchased.

Preparation

  • Protect the house, landscaping, concrete, and nearby surfaces.
  • Wash the porch and stairs and allow them to dry.
  • Scrape and sand loose or failing paint where needed.
  • Complete minor surface repairs and spot-prime bare or repaired white areas.

Preparation is limited to areas that need attention. Sound existing paint will remain in place.

Painting

  • Apply one tinted primer coat and one gray porch-and-floor finish coat to the porch floor, stair treads, and nosings.
  • Apply one complete white finish coat to the stair risers, stair railings, porch guardrail, balusters, posts, and caps.

Cleanup

  • Touch up painted surfaces as needed.
  • Remove masking and protection materials.
  • Clean the work area and complete a final walkthrough.

Not Included

  • Carpentry, structural repairs, rot repair, or wood replacement.
  • Full stripping or restoration.
  • Stair stringer faces, deck rim, lattice, porch ceiling, house siding or trim, front door, concrete, undersides, or framing.
  • Additional surfaces or repairs not listed above.

If unexpected repairs are found, we will discuss them before doing any additional work.

Before We Begin

  • Please clear furniture and personal items from the porch.
  • Approve the white and gray colors before materials are purchased.
  • Provide access to water, electricity, and an alternate entrance as needed.
  • Keep people and pets off freshly coated surfaces while they cure.

Price and Schedule

Front porch and entry stairs repaint: $1,450

We expect the work to take approximately two days with a two-person crew, depending on weather and drying time.

This estimate is valid for 30 days. Additional repairs or requested work will be discussed and priced separately.

The work is covered by the standard NE Portland Painters workmanship warranty; pre-existing coating failure and deteriorated wood are not covered.
