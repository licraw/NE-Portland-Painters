## Scope and Evidence

Included gray surfaces are the porch floor, stair tread tops, and tread nosings. Included white surfaces are the stair risers, stair railings, porch guardrail, balusters, posts, and caps. Stringer faces, deck rim, lattice, ceiling, house surfaces, door, concrete, undersides, and framing are excluded.

The ten supplied photographs were reviewed at full resolution. Counts labeled **direct** can be followed through the current stair photographs. Dimensions labeled **inferred** use nominal board widths, typical railing spacing, the 36-inch entry door, and repeated components as scale references.

### Porch floor

```text
9 modules x approximately 5.67 in = 51.0 in = 4.25 ft
Porch floor = 10.0 ft x 4.25 ft = 42.50 sq ft
```

The stairs meet the outside edge of the landing. The photographs do not show a rectangular floor opening extending into the porch, so **no stair-head deduction is taken**.
