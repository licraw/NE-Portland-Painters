# Customer Facing Estimate

**Project:** Exterior Painting  
**Property:** 2201 NE 58th Ave, Portland, OR 97213  
**Prepared:** 2026-06-22

## Project Summary

This estimate covers preparation and painting of the exterior painted surfaces on the 1.5-story bungalow at 2201 NE 58th Ave. The home has existing painted aluminum siding, painted trim, multiple gables, a covered front porch, rear entry/deck access, and a brick chimney.

The brick chimney is not included for painting.

## Scope of Work

### Surface Preparation

- Wash exterior painted siding and trim surfaces.
- Protect adjacent surfaces, roofing, windows, masonry, landscaping, walkways, and deck areas.
- Scrape loose and failing paint where present.
- Sand/feather localized failed areas as appropriate for the substrate.
- Spot prime bare or compromised areas as needed.
- Caulk typical open trim joints, siding/trim transitions, and minor gaps.
- Prepare front porch ceiling, including heavy scraping of existing peeling paint.

### Painting Scope

- Paint aluminum body siding with two finish coats.
- Paint painted window trim, door trim, fascia, soffits, corner boards, porch posts, and porch fascia.
- Paint front porch ceiling after preparation and priming.
- Paint front and exterior doors where currently painted, unless final color schedule excludes them.

### Repairs Included

This estimate includes a minor repair allowance for localized exterior paint-related repairs:

- Minor window sill/base repairs.
- Minor bondo repairs where localized deterioration is present.
- Minor wood patching associated with painting preparation.

Major carpentry replacement is not included.

## Exclusions

- Brick chimney painting, staining, or masonry coating.
- Major carpentry replacement.
- Structural repairs.
- Roof, gutter, or flashing repairs.
- Deck floor, deck railing, rear trellis/pergola, or fence painting unless added by written change order.
- Lead paint remediation or hazardous-material abatement.
- Hidden rot or moisture damage not visible during estimating.
- Color changes requiring more than the specified two finish coats.
