# Interior Painting Estimate

**Project:** 1707 SE Keanu Ct - Half Bath Repaint  
**Property:** 1707 SE Keanu Ct, Portland, OR 97267  
**Prepared:** 2026-07-26

## Project Summary

This estimate covers preparation and painting of the smaller main-floor half bath at 1707 SE Keanu Ct. Existing dark blue semi-gloss walls and ceiling will be repainted in a warm off-white/cream. The beadboard wainscoting, cap rail, baseboards, window trim, door casing, and entire door will be painted dark terracotta.

Unity R129 and Sugar Dust 0011 are inspiration colors only. Final colors will be confirmed before paint is purchased.

## Scope of Work

### Surface Preparation

- Protect floors and adjacent surfaces.
- Clean painted surfaces.
- Scuff sand glossy finishes as needed.
- Fill typical nail holes and minor surface imperfections.
- Spot prime repairs and apply bonding primer where required.
- Caulk minor trim joints and gaps as needed.

### Painting Scope

- Paint the ceiling and upper drywall with two finish coats.
- Paint the beadboard wainscoting, cap rail, baseboards, window trim, door casing, and entire six-panel door.
- Complete normal touch-ups, cleanup, and final walkthrough.

### Repairs Included

This estimate includes a minor repair allowance for typical paint preparation:

- Minor nail, anchor, and accessory-hole filling.
- Minor drywall patching.
- Minor caulking at typical open trim joints.

Major drywall repair, texture repair, or trim replacement is not included.

## Exclusions

- Major drywall repair, skim coating, or texture matching.
- Wallpaper removal.
- Water damage, mold, or mildew remediation.
- Carpentry, plumbing, or electrical work.
- Window repair or glass replacement.
- Removal or reinstallation of the vanity, sink, or toilet.
- Painting glass, plumbing fixtures, electrical devices, light fixtures, or the ventilation grille.
- Hidden damage not visible during estimating.
- Additional coats beyond normal coverage requirements.

## Total Estimate

**Total half bath painting estimate: $600**

This price is based on visible conditions and the stated scope. Final pricing may change if field inspection identifies hidden damage or customer-requested scope changes.
