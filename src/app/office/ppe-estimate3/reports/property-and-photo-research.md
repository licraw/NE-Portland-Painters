## Estimating Conclusion

The scope fits a one-painter, one-working-day production plan if repairs remain minor, the room stays empty, and normal drying conditions allow recoating. The small square footage is offset by five coating groups/surface types, a strong dark-to-light color change, glossy existing paint, beadboard grooves, a detailed six-panel door, and numerous cut-in points.
