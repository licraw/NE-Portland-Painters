## Primer Model

Bonding primer is included because the dark blue existing coating appears glossy/semi-gloss and the upper surfaces are changing to a much lighter color. The working plan is:

- Clean and scuff/degloss all glossy painted surfaces.
- Prime patched locations after sanding.
- Apply one continuous bonding-primer coat to the dark blue ceiling and upper drywall.
- Apply bonding primer to the existing wainscoting, trim, and door as needed for adhesion and color control.
- Carry one gallon of bonding primer; field conditions determine whether every lower surface requires a continuous coat or a combination of full and targeted priming.
