This shed should not be modeled like a gable-roof structure. It has a single-slope shed roof, meaning:

- The front wall is the tallest wall.
- The rear wall is lower.
- The two side elevations are trapezoids, not rectangles.
- There are no triangular gable ends to add.
- The front has a large overhang with visible soffit/eave underside.
- Side and rear eaves are meaningful dark-blue scope items.
- Main wall siding is light blue.
- Trim, fascia, eaves, soffits, door, and roof-edge detailing are dark blue.

- Front horizontal lap siding

## Surface-Specific Prep Notes

### Horizontal Front Lap Siding

Main wall field to be painted light blue. Likely smoother and more uniform than the side/rear. Scuff where needed and spot prime bare or stained areas. Mask door, windows, glass, and deck/pavers carefully.

### Vertical Rough Panel Siding

Main wall field to be painted light blue. Appears weathered with dirt, staining, and uneven existing coating. More absorbent than smooth siding.

- May require heavier washing/scrubbing.
- Spot prime exposed wood, raw fibers, and thin/failing coating areas.
- Spraying may need back-brushing or back-rolling to work paint into texture.

### New / Raw Wood Fascia and Eave Blocking

Dark blue. Should be primed before finish paint. Raw wood fascia will absorb more paint than previously painted surfaces. End grain and seams should receive extra attention.

### Soffits / Eaves

Dark blue. Includes front overhang, side overhangs, and assumed rear eave. Vented soffit strip should be painted carefully to avoid clogging vent openings. Mask adjacent roof/drip-edge elements unless they are intentionally included.

### Door and Trim

Dark blue. Front door is a distinct dark-blue scope item. Door trim, window trim, corner boards, and base/skirt trim should all be treated as dark blue under the updated color rule.

Confirm whether white vinyl/aluminum window frames themselves are to remain white or be masked off.

## Exclusions / Confirm Before Final Estimate

Unless specifically added to the scope, exclude:

- Metal roof panels
- Metal drip edge
- Gutters
- Downspouts
- Glass
- Window screens
- Pavers/deck tiles
- Adjacent fence or house surfaces
- Interior studio surfaces

## Verification Items

Before finalizing the estimate, verify:

- Actual rear wall height.
- Whether the rear eave/soffit is included and its depth.
- Whether white window frames are to stay white or be painted dark blue.
- Whether metal drip edge/gutters/downspouts are excluded or painted.
- Whether the front raw wood fascia has already been primed.
- Whether all lower base/skirt boards are included as dark blue.
- Whether any side/rear small vents or openings should be deducted.
