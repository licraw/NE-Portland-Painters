# Exterior Painting Estimate

**Project:** Backyard Shed / Studio Lounge Exterior Painting  
**Property:** 7610 SE Harrison St, Portland, OR 97215  
**Prepared:** 2026-07-01

## Project Summary

This estimate covers preparation and exterior painting of the backyard shed/studio structure at 7610 SE Harrison St. The structure includes front horizontal lap siding, side and rear rough vertical panel siding, a front door, trim, soffits/eaves, fascia, corner boards, base/skirt trim, and exposed roof-edge details.

The main wall siding/body fields will be painted **light blue**. The eaves, soffits, fascia, roof-edge boards, exposed blocking, front door, door trim, window trim, corner boards, base/skirt trim, and other detail surfaces will be painted **dark blue**.

## Scope of Work

### Preparation

- Wash exterior siding, trim, eaves, soffits, fascia, and door surfaces.
- Protect adjacent roof edges, windows, screens, glass, deck/paver areas, landscaping, and nearby surfaces.
- Scrape or brush loose dirt, failing coating, and weathered surface material where present.
- Sand or scuff transitions as needed for sound paint adhesion.
- Spot prime bare, raw, weathered, stained, or compromised areas.
- Prime raw/new fascia, roof-edge boards, exposed eave blocking, and other raw wood trim before finish painting.
- Caulk typical open trim joints, siding/trim transitions, corner-board joints, and minor gaps where appropriate.
- Prepare vented soffit areas carefully to avoid clogging vent openings.

### Painting

- Apply two finish coats to the light-blue main wall siding/body fields.
- Apply two finish coats to the dark-blue eaves, soffits, fascia, roof-edge boards, exposed blocking, front door, door trim, window trim, corner boards, and base/skirt trim.
- Mask glass, screens, and window frame components unless those components are specifically added to the paint scope.

### Cleanup

- Remove masking and protection materials.
- Touch up as needed.
- Clean up paint-related debris and leave the work area orderly.

## Exclusions

- Metal roof panels, metal drip edge, gutters, or downspouts.
- Glass, window screens, and interior window components.
- Deck tiles, pavers, concrete, landscaping, fencing, and adjacent house/garage surfaces.
- Interior studio/lounge surfaces.
- Major carpentry replacement, structural repairs, roof repairs, gutter repairs, or flashing repairs.
- Lead paint remediation or hazardous-material abatement.
- Hidden rot or moisture damage not visible during estimating.
- Additional coats required by major color changes beyond the specified two finish coats.

## Schedule

Expected project duration is approximately **2-3 working days**, depending on weather, drying conditions, and final field conditions.

## Total Estimate

**Total exterior painting estimate: $2,775**

This price is based on the visible conditions, project photos, measured shed/studio dimensions, and the scope described above. Final pricing may change if field inspection identifies hidden rot, moisture damage, failing substrate, inaccessible areas, raw wood beyond the visible fascia/eave scope, or customer-selected scope additions.
