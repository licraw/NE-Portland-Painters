<p>There is a vast selection of products on the market. It’s no wonder homeowners often feel overwhelmed when it comes time to buy paint for their next home improvement project. Hiring a reputable painting contractor like NE Portland Painters has some pros. That contractor should also serve as a wealth of knowledge concerning the wide variety of available products. They will also select the most appropriate product based on your needs.  However, if you choose to go the DIY route, here are a few tips:</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>Don’t skimp on product.</b></span>  </h4>



<p>While it may be tempting to save money on the cost of paint, it’s likely that you will end up paying it back in labor.  Inexpensive, low-quality paint may take twice as many coats to cover as a higher-quality product.  This will cause your project to take longer than necessary. You may find that you didn’t save much money at all, since you will have to buy extra paint.  Not only do higher-quality paints offer better coverage, they are also more durable, holding up to scrubbing/washing.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>Consider visiting a local paint store</b></span> </h4>



<p>Local paint stores offer a wider range of support rather than a general home-improvement store.  Employees at the paint store are far more likely to have expert product knowledge. This is because they are usually sales representatives for a particular brand of paint.  These employees work with contractors like NE Portland Painters every day. They&#8217;re here to offer helpful suggestions for your next project.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><b>You may want to buy primer</b></span></h4>



<p> Be cautious when you choose a “paint and primer in one”. Sometimes it is actually better to apply a separate coat of primer before painting.  This especially applies if the surface is being painted for the first time (i.e. raw wood) or you are trying to achieve a drastic color change.  A little-known fact is that you can have your primer tinted to the desired color, to maximize coverage.</p>



<p>Remember that there is no one-size-fits-all product or process for any project. Every job and homeowner have unique needs. It is always recommended to seek out the advice of a professional whenever possible.  Hiring a contractor like NE Portland Painters can save you the time and headache of completing your painting project on your own.</p>
