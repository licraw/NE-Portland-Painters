<p>It can be a difficult journey finding the right paint for your home, that&#8217;s why we&#8217;re here to help! As 2025 unfolds the common theme is color trends are turning to warm, comforting hues that invite calm and creativity into our living spaces. In this blog post we&#8217;ll look at what leading color authorities like Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, and Pantone have selected as their Color of the Year, providing a glimpse into the design trends that will shape interiors in the months ahead.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Benjamin Moore</strong></h2>



<p>Benjamin Moore has selected <strong>Cinnamon Slate 2113-40</strong> as its Color of the Year for 2025. This versatile shade is a rich blend of plum and brown, offering a soft yet sophisticated feel. The muted tone is designed to create a sense of comfort and familiarity, ideal for cozy living rooms, restful bedrooms, or inviting home offices.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image is-resized"><img decoding="async" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXetvoy5ei9jyuhuaMt67vRPxAQXl55e7V7DmPMV0crkUBU3f2gALPeZdPCg1jBngj9XKEFSCADKTNqp3AMJ7HA3C6fV3rIbvtWuCPswDczU78yaA69jKKey1x725bQN-csPXFbUPQ?key=Hgp9ICTxvDH2N4Th3G7cIHxg" alt="" style="width:354px;height:auto"/></figure>



<p>Cinnamon Slate pairs beautifully with complementary hues from Benjamin Moore&#8217;s 2025 palette, such as the soft pink of Tissue Pink or the deep richness of Leather Saddle Brown. This flexibility makes it an excellent choice for those seeking both warmth and elegance in their spaces.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Sherwin Williams</strong></h2>



<p>Instead of choosing a single shade, Sherwin-Williams is embracing variety with its <strong>2025 Color Capsule</strong> — a curated collection of nine versatile shades that offer a complete color story.</p>



<p>The nine shades from this palette include:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Grounded</strong> &#8211; a deep, earthy brown perfect for grounding a space.</li>



<li><strong>Sunbleached</strong> &#8211; a soft neutral that’s deeper than white but lighter than gray, ideal for calm and airy spaces.</li>



<li><strong>Chartreuse</strong> &#8211; a vibrant yellow-green that energizes interiors.</li>



<li><strong>Bosc Pear</strong> &#8211; a rich, golden hue that adds warmth to any room.</li>



<li><strong>White Snow</strong> &#8211; a bright, clean white for crisp accents or freshening up spaces.</li>



<li><strong>Rain Cloud</strong> &#8211; a moody gray-blue ideal for creating drama and depth.</li>



<li><strong>Clove</strong> &#8211; a nearly black shade of brown that adds richness and sophistication.</li>



<li><strong>Malabar</strong> &#8211; a warm, creamy beige that offers a soothing foundation.</li>



<li><strong>Mauve Finery</strong> &#8211; a soft, delicate mauve that brings elegance and charm.</li>
</ul>



<figure class="wp-block-image"><img decoding="async" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdd2B86TFLnGQYNILBB0E45ZF1n12J34jlpf3jTOiCR6WA6DDWLIuKVEuLBbqb12dcNIi5OSmkTy9Too-JGyDHEjk5gAP8i564Jco8_XB6c4Inws2i-uwp9G1Hq1dnZwfR1fqwe?key=Hgp9ICTxvDH2N4Th3G7cIHxg" alt=""/></figure>



<p>This diverse palette makes it easy for homeowners to mix and match tones, creating layered and dynamic designs. Whether you prefer minimalism or bold expression, the Color Capsule offers something for everyone.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Pantone</strong></h2>



<p>Pantone&#8217;s Color of the Year for 2025, <strong>PANTONE 17-1230 Mocha Mousse</strong>, is a rich, warming brown that evokes indulgence and natural beauty. Inspired by comforting elements like coffee, chocolate, and warm earth tones, Mocha Mousse strikes a balance between elegance and relaxation.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image is-resized"><img decoding="async" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfw0IrAsFGJ_Tn7VwEMwoyN-qZt8V51WwRtXUZ-jNNGYZKbJdQqRTOVfvvK0kOH8bHwRMTfs0g9eP17EQ1zTcKjRgmBS_0w2f8nEAQm7Xgm4Ymktxfps5AKto1lL9st3wAhX-Hj?key=Hgp9ICTxvDH2N4Th3G7cIHxg" alt="" style="width:260px;height:auto"/></figure>



<p>According to Leatrice Eiseman, Executive Director of the Pantone Color Institute, this shade embodies &#8220;thoughtful indulgence&#8221; and brings a sense of calm and timelessness. Whether featured on walls, furniture, or textiles, Mocha Mousse adds depth and character to any space.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Bringing 2025&#8217;s Colors into Your Home</strong></h3>



<p>This year&#8217;s top colors are all about comfort, warmth, and versatility. For those seeking a cozy and inviting atmosphere, Cinnamon Slate and Mocha Mousse provide rich, earthy foundations. Meanwhile, Sherwin-Williams&#8217; Color Capsule offers a broader spectrum for those who love to experiment with color combinations.</p>



<p>Whether you&#8217;re planning a full room makeover or just looking to add a fresh accent wall, these 2025 colors offer endless opportunities to refresh your home with style and personality.</p>
