<p>Earlier this year, we wrote about how painting the exterior of your home is a cost-effective way to add value to your home. Now that it is winter, we will be discussing simple ways to add value to your home by updating the interior! This list includes some of our favorite low-cost methods. These options are inexpensive, but all can have a big impact on the overall appearance of a space, and leave a lasting impression.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">1. Changing light fixtures</span><br>This is one of the easiest upgrades you can make. &nbsp;We recommend replacing your current light fixtures with something brighter, more modern, and more energy-efficient. &nbsp;Properly placed lighting can add warmth and friendliness to a room, and energy-efficient options will even save you money.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">2. Updating cabinets</span><br>One of the most economical options is to replace cabinet hardware. <a href="http://www.houzz.com/cabinet-hardware" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cabinet hardware</a> is generally inexpensive, and you can easily install it yourself!  This is one of the smaller changes on the list, but it can make a dramatic difference in the overall appearance of a room!   You may also update your cabinets by (re)painting them. We really like this <a href="https://www.mlcampbell.com/products/categories/pigmented_topcoats/solvent_borne/postcatalyzed/resistant" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">conversion varnish from ML Campbell</a>.  You can see the product in action on a recent cabinetry project by NE Portland Painters <a href="/blog/cheap-ways-to-upgrade-your-interior/cabinets-1024x879.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">here</a>.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">3. Adding new blinds</span><br>New blinds (or curtains) can fade, wear out, and accumulate dirt over time. &nbsp;Update old, standard blinds or curtains to add personality to a room. &nbsp;An alternative is to replace your blinds with shutters. Find some inspiration <a href="http://www.houzz.com/blinds" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">here</a>.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">4. Adding crown molding</span><br>Adding <a href="http://www.houzz.com/crown-molding" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">crown molding</a> is inexpensive and adds elegance to a room. &nbsp;Another great option is to freshen up existing trim by repainting it. &nbsp;A professional painting contractor like NE Portland Painters can repaint old trim or even paint newly installed trim.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">5. Replacing electrical outlet covers<br></span>This is another subtle change that can add style to a room. &nbsp;With the wide variety of options available, you can pick an outlet cover that complements existing decor! &nbsp;It may seem like a small change, it can make a surprisingly big impression.</p>



<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">6.&nbsp;Painting walls</span><br>Of course! Painting walls is the most obvious and often most dramatic way to change the mood of a room. &nbsp;A reputable contractor like NE Portland Painters will make sure to cover and protect your furniture &amp; surrounding areas and thoroughly prepare all surfaces for a clean, long-lasting finish.</p>
