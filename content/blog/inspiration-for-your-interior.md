<p>With the exterior season beginning to wind down, you might be thinking about that interior painting project you have meant to get started on. &nbsp;Choosing colors can be a daunting task. After all, the inside of your home is where you will likely spend most of your time, and the mood created by your paint selections should make <em>you</em> happy!</p>



<p>Keep in mind, however, that if you are looking to do a complete design overhaul, paint color is usually the last element that you should choose. Only after selecting furniture, decor, etc. should you decide upon a paint color for your project, to make sure that the color flows well with the rest of the room and design selections you have made.</p>

With this tool, you may use either&nbsp;sample photos or your own uploaded photos to virtually change paint colors.
