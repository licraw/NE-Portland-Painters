<p class="has-text-align-left">Accent walls are a great way to add a creative spin on an average interior paint project! The possibilities are ENDLESS! Below we provide tips and tricks for creating a show-stopping accent wall.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Why are accent walls important?</h4>



<p>Accent walls can have many functions that can make them fantastic design features of your home or space. Accent walls work to&#8230;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Highlight an architectural feature, such as fireplaces, sloped walls, and floor-to-ceiling cabinets or shelves.</li>



<li>Bring interest to a transition space, such as a hallway, door, or ceiling.</li>



<li>Designate a space for a purpose such as a bar or breakfast nook.</li>



<li>Add dimension to a room. If your style is more simplistic, an accent wall can add depth and excitement to a room.</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Do&#8217;s and Don&#8217;ts</h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>DO be creative. You can do more than just paint your accent wall. You can utilize wallpaper, stencils, paneling, etc. to create your accent wall.</li>



<li>DO make sure your accent wall complements the color of other walls in the room. Use the accent wall as a continuation of your decor&#8217;s color scheme, or use this project as a time to reinvent a space.</li>



<li>DON&#8217;T shy away from bold colors or patterns. Find a color that fits your personality and that you will be happy within the long run. Going with a darker neutral is safe, but takes away from the fun possibilities of an accent wall.</li>



<li>DON&#8217;T forget to take into account the texture, architectural features, and light exposure of your accent wall. You want to choose an accent wall design that will complement the physical attributions of space.</li>
</ul>
