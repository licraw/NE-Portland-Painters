<p>Another year has flown by and it&#8217;s time to share a few of our 2024 projects. We&#8217;ve had so many amazing clients this year and are extremely grateful for the support we&#8217;ve received. Our crews worked hard this year, and the results have been some amazing painting projects! It would be impossible to showcase every single project we completed, so here are a few of our favorites.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Exterior Painting Project</h2>



<p>One of our standout projects in 2024 was this incredible exterior transformation. Before our painting crew could get started, our skilled carpenters tackled the critical task of replacing rotting siding boards on the home and garage. Their meticulous work set the stage for our painters to step in and complete this impressive 500-hour project.</p>



<p>What made this project particularly unique was the homeowner’s stunning garden, which was located very close to the home. While we always take care to protect our clients&#8217; yards and gardens, this project required extra attention due to the extensive array of plants surrounding the house. Our team carefully planned and executed every step to ensure that no damage came to the lush greenery, allowing the garden to perfectly frame the refreshed exterior.</p>



<p>The result? A beautifully repainted home that stands out even more against the backdrop of vibrant plants. We’re thrilled with how it all came together and proud to deliver results that enhance both the home and its natural surroundings.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Interior Painting Project</h2>



<p><br>This interior repaint project was completed just in time for the holiday season, wrapping up in mid-December. The client had recently purchased this home and wanted to refresh nearly every wall with a fresh coat of paint before moving in and finalizing renovations.</p>



<p>Timing was important for this project. Our crew worked efficiently to complete the entire home in just two week!  After about 400 hours of painting, we delivered a stunning transformation that was exactly what the client had envisioned for their updated space. The end result was a home that felt fresh, clean, and ready for its next chapter. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Exterior Painting &amp; Staining Project</h2>



<p>We completed this exterior project early on in the summer of 2024. This was an amazing way to kick off the exterior season and get our crews back outside! This project was completed by our lead James and his crew. </p>



<p>For this home, we painted the siding in a two-tone combination of SW 6385 Dover White and SW 7020 Black Fox for a striking yet balanced look. The wood on the deck, front, side, and back was stained with Sikkens Cedar, maintaining a warm, natural finish. We love the colors that these clients chose and couldn&#8217;t think of a more perfect combination to create the modern vibe shown above. </p>
