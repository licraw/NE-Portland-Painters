<p>NE Portland Painters recently completed a volunteer project for Mary&#8217;s Place Seattle! We are grateful to have had the opportunity to contribute to an amazing nonprofit like this one. As stated on their <a href="https://www.marysplaceseattle.org/mission">website</a>, &#8220;The Mary’s Place mission is to ensure that no child sleeps outside by centering equity and opportunity for women and families.&#8221; We are always on the lookout for ways that we can give back to the community. When we heard that Mary&#8217;s Place was in need of a new TikTok wall we couldn&#8217;t wait to help!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Painting for Mary&#8217;s Place</h2>



<p>When we heard that Mary&#8217;s Place was needing assistance in painting a wall for their social media content  we couldn&#8217;t wait to get involved. NE Portland Painters owner Jeff and senior crew lead Kevin showed up ready to complete the perfect paint job.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What is a TikTok Wall? </h2>



<p>A TikTok wall is a wall painted green that can act as a green screen for videos. Businesses and non-profits who utilize social media as a part of their marketing strategy may be familiar with the logistics that go into creating one of these videos. You need to have a good backdrop, a script, and a plan to make sure you target specific keywords so that you show up in front of the right people. Mary&#8217;s Place was looking for a way to simplify their content creation and have a backdrop available to them in house. That&#8217;s what led them to the idea of installing a TikTok wall and where NE Portland Painters was able to help.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Process</h2>



<p>The process for painting their TikTok wall started with figuring out what green paint would be the best choice for the job. After some research, we found the perfect bright green that would work well to act as a green screen for their videos. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="/blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-1024x576.jpeg" alt="" class="wp-image-4225" srcset="/blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-1024x576.jpeg 1024w, /blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-300x169.jpeg 300w, /blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-768x432.jpeg 768w, /blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-100x56.jpeg 100w, /blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g-1140x641.jpeg 1140w, /blog/june-2024-volunteer-project/down-net_http20240624-68-ijd66g.jpeg 1440w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Our crew lead Kevin putting the finishing touches on the wall. </figcaption></figure>



<p>The painting process was fairly simple due to the fact that they only needed us to paint a small section of the wall. Jeff and Kevin showed up early to start prepping the wall before the bright paint could be applied. After taping off the section, they applied a coat of primer and then waited for that to dry. After that, all it took as applying two coats of this fun green before they were finished!</p>
