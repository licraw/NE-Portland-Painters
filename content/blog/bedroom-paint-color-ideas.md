<p>Selecting the perfect paint color can be challenging, no matter the space. We of all people understand the time and energy that goes into painting a room, so choosing the right color is crucial to avoid future repaints just to fix the aesthetics. When it comes to your bedroom, the pressure is even higher—you&#8217;ll spend countless hours in this space, and you want it to be just right. In this post, we&#8217;ll share some of our favorite bedroom paint color ideas from trusted brands like Sherwin Williams and Benjamin Moore to help you create your ideal sanctuary.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Greek Villa</h5>



<p>When searching for timeless neutral paint colors for a bedroom, Sherwin Williams Greek Villa is likely to be at the top of your list. This creamy white hue offers a classic and versatile backdrop that endures over time. Whether you choose to paint your walls, trim, and ceiling in Greek Villa, you can easily introduce vibrant accents through other bedroom elements to add personal flair. Greek Villa is also a popular choice for those planning to move in the near future, as its neutral tone can enhance property value and present the space as a blank canvas for potential buyers.</p>

</div>


<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Slumber Sloth</h5>



<p>Sherwin Williams Slumber Sloth is another excellent neutral color to consider for your bedroom. With its slightly warmer tone compared to Greek Villa, it offers a cozy yet timeless appeal. Slumber Sloth maintains the same value for new homeowners, making it an ideal choice for those looking to enhance their space while also appealing to potential buyers. Its inviting warmth creates a serene atmosphere, perfect for a restful bedroom environment.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Snowbound</h5>



<p>Sherwin Williams Snowbound is one of the most popular color selections we see at NE Portland Painters. Its crisp, clean finish makes it an excellent choice for those who prefer to keep their walls neutral and sleek. Snowbound&#8217;s versatility doesn&#8217;t end with walls; it&#8217;s also a favorite for ceilings and trim. Many of our clients choose Snowbound for these areas, pairing it with a contrasting wall color to create a fresh, cohesive look. Its bright, inviting nature enhances any space, making it a dependable option for various design styles and preferences. Whether used throughout an entire room or as an accent, Snowbound consistently delivers a timeless and elegant finish.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Upward</h5>



<p>Sherwin Williams declared Upward as their 2024 Color of the Year, anticipating a rise in popularity for lighter hues. Over the past few years, blue shades have steadily gained traction in interior design, and Upward embodies this trend beautifully. Its light, airy tone introduces a calming energy, making it an ideal choice for bedrooms. Whether used throughout the room or as an accent, Upward transforms any space into a serene retreat, perfectly blending tranquility and style. </p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Pashmina</h5>



<p>Benjamin Moore&#8217;s Pashmina is a solid neutral choice with a touch of flair, making it ideal for bedroom walls. Described on their website as a sophisticated neutral, Pashmina offers a balanced blend of warmth and elegance. This versatile shade can complement various design styles, adding a timeless and stylish touch to any bedroom. Its subtle undertones provide a cozy yet refined ambiance, perfect for creating a serene retreat where you can unwind and relax. Whether you&#8217;re aiming for a modern, traditional, or eclectic look, Pashmina seamlessly adapts, making it a dependable choice for your bedroom decor.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Constellation</h5>



<p>Benjamin Moore&#8217;s Constellation (AF-540) is a serene and sophisticated paint color, perfect for creating a peaceful ambiance in the bedroom. This soft, airy blue has subtle gray undertones, which give it a balanced, calming effect without being too cool or overpowering. Constellation&#8217;s versatility makes it ideal for pairing with various design elements, from crisp white linens to natural wood accents, allowing it to seamlessly blend into both modern and traditional bedroom decor. Its light, tranquil hue promotes relaxation, making it an excellent choice for a restful retreat.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Blue Nova</h5>



<p>Benjamin Moore&#8217;s Blue Nova is a rich and inviting shade that adds depth and character to any bedroom. Blue Nova was actually deemed Benjamin Moore&#8217;s <a href="https://www.soundpaintingsolutions.com/2024-colors-of-the-year/">2024 color of the year</a> due to its popularity. This deep blue color is just enough warmth to create a cozy, enveloping atmosphere. Blue Nova works beautifully as an accent wall or as the main color in a bedroom, offering a perfect backdrop for a variety of decor styles from classic to contemporary. Its hue pairs well with metallic accents, neutral tones, and natural textures, making it the perfect choice for anyone looking to make a bold yet refined statement.</p>

<h5 class="wp-block-heading">Hazy Lilac</h5>



<p>Hazy Lilac is a soft, dreamy color that brings a little elegance to any bedroom. Hazy Lilac is perfect for creating a tranquil and soothing environment, making it an ideal choice for a restful bedroom retreat. Its subtle, romantic hue pairs beautifully with soft whites, neutral grays, and natural wood finishes, allowing for a variety of design possibilities that evoke a sense of calm and comfort.</p>
