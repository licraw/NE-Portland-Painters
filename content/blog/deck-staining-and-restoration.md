<p>Decks are a summer staple, with the promise of the season bringing backyard barbecues and outdoor gatherings. As summer approaches, you may be considering refinishing your deck. Decks are an area of your home requiring extra care, as they are subject to heavy traffic, harsh weather, and moisture. A quality stain provides your deck with an extra layer of protection against the elements. If possible, you should stain your deck at least once a year as normal maintenance. This kind of maintenance is especially important in a rainy area like Seattle.</p>



<p>Whether your deck could use another stain coating, has recently been built, or is in need of a full restoration, hiring a qualified stain contractor will ensure that the job is completed to industry standards.&nbsp; A professional will also ensure that the surface of the deck is cleaned and prepared adequately prior to application of the stain.</p>



<p>NE Portland Painters, LLC is a Benjamin Moore certified stain contractor, specifically trained in the application of Benjamin Moore Arborcoat® products. We can help you evaluate the condition of your deck and select the most appropriate products for your project.</p>
