<p>When it comes to painting your home or commercial space, choosing the right color is only half the battle. The finish you select plays a major role in the final look, durability, and overall performance of your paint job. At NE Portland Painters, we often guide our clients through these decisions to ensure they get the best results possible. Here’s a breakdown of the most common paint finishes, how they differ, and when to use each.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-wide"/>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Flat / Matte Finish</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Ceilings, low-traffic walls, bedrooms</p>



<p>Flat finishes offer a smooth, non-reflective surface that helps hide imperfections on walls and ceilings. They create a soft, velvety look that’s ideal for older homes or spaces with surface blemishes. However, flat paints are less durable and harder to clean, making them better suited for areas with minimal wear and tear.</p>

<p><strong>Pros:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Excellent at hiding surface flaws</li>



<li>Rich, elegant appearance</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Difficult to clean</li>



<li>Can scuff easily</li>
</ul>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-wide"/>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Eggshell Finish</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms</p>



<p>Eggshell has a slight sheen—just enough to be more washable than flat, but still subdued enough to keep imperfections from standing out. It’s a popular all-around choice for interior walls in residential spaces.</p>

<p><strong>Pros:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>More washable than flat</li>



<li>Subtle sheen adds depth without glare</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Still not great for high-moisture or high-traffic areas</li>
</ul>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-wide"/>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Satin Finish</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Kitchens, bathrooms, hallways, kids&#8217; rooms</p>



<p>Satin finishes strike a balance between softness and durability. They are ideal for high-traffic areas and rooms that need frequent cleaning. With a bit more gloss, satin can reveal surface imperfections more than eggshell, but it offers excellent stain resistance.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="682" src="/blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/New-Project-27.png.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-5144" style="width:686px;height:auto" srcset="/blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/New-Project-27.png.webp 1024w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/New-Project-27.png-300x200.webp 300w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/New-Project-27.png-768x512.webp 768w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/New-Project-27.png-100x67.webp 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p><strong>Pros:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Durable and easy to clean</li>



<li>Great for moisture-prone or high-traffic areas</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>May highlight wall imperfections</li>
</ul>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-wide"/>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4. Semi-Gloss Finish</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Trim, doors, cabinets, bathrooms</p>



<p>Semi-gloss paint has a noticeable shine that makes it ideal for accent areas that need durability and frequent cleaning. It resists moisture well and provides a nice contrast to flatter finishes used on walls.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full is-resized"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="912" height="512" src="/blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/What-is-Semi-Gloss-Paint-and-When-to-Use-It.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-5145" style="width:696px;height:auto" srcset="/blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/What-is-Semi-Gloss-Paint-and-When-to-Use-It.jpg 912w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/What-is-Semi-Gloss-Paint-and-When-to-Use-It-300x168.jpg 300w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/What-is-Semi-Gloss-Paint-and-When-to-Use-It-768x431.jpg 768w, /blog/the-difference-between-paint-finishes-and-when-to-use-each/What-is-Semi-Gloss-Paint-and-When-to-Use-It-100x56.jpg 100w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 912px) 100vw, 912px" /></figure>



<p><strong>Pros:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Very durable and moisture-resistant</li>



<li>Easy to wipe clean</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Will show brush strokes and wall flaws more easily</li>
</ul>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-wide"/>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>5. High-Gloss Finish</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Furniture, cabinets, trim (in modern designs)</p>



<p>This is the shiniest and most durable finish. High-gloss paint is perfect for statement pieces or ultra-sleek designs. However, it requires a perfectly prepped surface to avoid highlighting every bump and flaw.</p>

<p><strong>Pros:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Ultra-durable and stain-resistant</li>



<li>Highly reflective and modern looking</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Very unforgiving on imperfect surfaces</li>



<li>Harder to apply evenly</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How to Choose the Right Finish</strong></h3>



<p>When we work with clients at NE Portland Painters, we always factor in:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Room usage and traffic</strong></li>



<li><strong>Desired aesthetic</strong></li>



<li><strong>Lighting conditions</strong></li>



<li><strong>Surface condition</strong></li>
</ul>
