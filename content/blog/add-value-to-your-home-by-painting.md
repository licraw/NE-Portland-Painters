<p><br>If you are looking to sell your home in the near future, consider hiring a professional painting contractor. A fresh, quality paint job has the potential to add thousands of dollars in value to your home. An attractive exterior paint job may also entice potential buyers and create a great first impression of your property.</p>



<p>Before you decide to paint, however, be sure to evaluate the condition of the areas that you plan to paint. This especially applies to exterior surfaces. While a fresh paint job can do wonders in terms of appearance, it is important to note that it is not a substitute for repairing damaged areas, especially wooden surfaces where dryrot may be present.</p>



<p>When planning your painting project, choose neutral colors over bold colors, as these will be pleasant to most people. When considering the opinions of potential buyers, opting for an all-white paint job may seem like a good idea. However, it may have an adverse effect, appearing bland and boring to viewers. A variety of soft, neutral colors is recommended, as this will appeal to most people and add uniqueness to your home.</p>
