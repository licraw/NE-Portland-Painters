<p>We’re thrilled to announce that NE Portland Painters has been honored with the 2024 Spirit of Service Award from the Master Builders Association of King and Snohomish Counties! This award highlights our commitment to giving back to the community. We are extremely grateful for this recognition and we look forward to participating in more MBA events in 2025. </p>



<p>Giving back to our community has always been a core value at NE Portland Painters. We take pride in being an active participant in initiatives that improve lives and strengthen neighborhoods. One of our favorite programs is the MBA’s <em><a href="https://www.mbaks.com/community/painting-a-better-tomorrow">Paint a Better Tomorrow</a></em>. This program brings local businesses together to serve and uplift our community through impactful projects.</p>

<p>This year, we participated in a <em>Paint a Better Tomorrow</em> event at the Bellevue Boys &amp; Girls Club. Our team of 11 painters worked together to refresh and brighten the space. We hope that our contributions create a more welcoming environment for the children and teens who use the facility. To learn more about our involvement in painting the Bellevue Boys &amp; Girls Club, <a href="https://www.soundpaintingsolutions.com/november-2024-volunteer-project/">read our blog post here</a>. </p>



<p>We extend our heartfelt thanks to the Master Builders Association for organizing these events and recognizing the efforts of businesses that share a commitment to community service. We’re also deeply grateful to our incredible team and the clients who support us—your trust allows us to dedicate time and resources to causes that matter.</p>



<p>At NE Portland Painters, we believe that every brushstroke has the power to create change. Awards like the Spirit of Service remind us why we do what we do and inspire us to continue finding ways to give back. Thank you to everyone who supports community-focused initiatives—together, we can build a brighter future for all.</p>
